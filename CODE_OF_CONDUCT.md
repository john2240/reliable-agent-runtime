# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, technically rigorous place to
collaborate.

Contributors must communicate constructively, critique ideas rather than
people, respect privacy, and avoid harassment or discriminatory behavior.
Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct issues privately through the security/contact channel described
in the repository. Reports will be handled confidentially and in good faith.
